Playing

How do I start?

Open the app and pick a buddy — that’s it. There’s no account or sign-in. On the home screen, choose a subject tab (Math, Letters & Reading, Our World) or “All”, then tap any game to play a round of 5, 8 or 10 questions.

How do the three kinds of questions work?

Tap it: tap the answer you think is right.

Type it: when a question shows a number pad, type your answer and tap Check. There’s nothing to guess from, so it builds real skill. Many games mix tap and type questions.

Order it: when a question shows cards, tap them in the right order, then tap Check. Tap a placed card to take it back, or Reset to start over.

What happens when I get one wrong?

You always get a second try, with a hint. If the second try is also wrong, the app shows the right answer and a How to solve it explanation, step by step. Tap Next when you’re ready.

Missed questions come back a little later in the same round, so the new idea sticks. The app also adjusts itself: a streak of right answers makes questions a bit harder, and a couple of misses makes them a bit easier.

What are Hint and Repeat?

Hint shows (and reads) a clue without giving the answer away. Repeat reads the question aloud again.

What is K+ Challenge?

Numbers to 20, trickier sounds and words, and bonus science. Turn it on in Settings → Game Play or with the chip in Quick settings on the home screen. It’s part of the Full Adventure Unlock.

What is the timer?

Turn on Settings → Timer challenge to race the clock on each question. Type-it and order-it questions get extra time automatically. The timer is off by default.

What is the “Game of the Day”?

Each day one premium game is free to play for the whole day — look for the FREE TODAY badge. It changes at midnight on your device.

Read-aloud & voices

How do I turn reading aloud on or off?

Use the Read it chip in Quick settings on the home screen, or Settings → Read questions aloud. It’s on by default, because young learners are still building reading skills.

Can I change the voice or how fast it reads?

Yes. Open Settings → Voice and choose any English voice on your device, then tap Test to hear it. “Automatic” picks the best-quality voice available. Use Reading speed to make it slower or faster.

How do I get a more natural-sounding voice?

Apple offers free, high-quality voices. On the iPhone or iPad open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, pick a voice marked Enhanced or Premium and download it. Then choose it in the app under Settings → Voice (or leave it on Automatic — it picks the best one for you).

I can’t hear anything.

Check that the device volume is up and that Sound is on in the app’s Quick settings. If sound effects play but questions aren’t read, turn on Read it. On some devices the ring/silent switch also mutes app sounds.

Rewards

How do coins, XP and levels work?

Every right answer earns XP and coins. Finishing a round adds bonus coins (more for a perfect round). XP fills your level bar; each new level earns a new title and bonus coins. There are 28 achievements to unlock.

What is the daily goal and streak?

Earn 8 stars (right answers) in a day to complete Today’s Goal. Play on consecutive days to grow your streak — and open the free Daily Gift in the Reward Shop every day. Longer streaks mean bigger gifts.

What can I get in the Reward Shop?

Spend coins on 18 buddies, 8 color themes that reskin the whole app, a Mystery Box, and power-ups:

  • 50 / 50 — removes two wrong answers
  • Time Freeze — adds 15 seconds when the timer is on
  • Streak Shield — saves your streak from one miss
  • Coin Doubler — doubles your coins for the next round

Power-ups appear under the question when you own them.

For grown-ups

How do I open the Grown-Ups area?

Tap Grown-Ups on the home screen and answer the question shown in words (for example “seven times eight” → type 56). This parental gate keeps children out of the dashboard and away from purchases.

What does the dashboard show?

Level, total correct answers, accuracy, rounds played, day streak and perfect rounds; Focus Areas — the games where accuracy is lowest (after enough practice to be meaningful); and a skill-by-skill breakdown for every game, grouped by subject. You can also see and restore purchases there.

Which skills does the app cover?

22 games aligned to kindergarten standards (Common Core math and ELA, NGSS science). See the full list of games below.

Is it OK for more than one child to use the same device?

The app keeps one set of progress per device. Siblings can take turns, but they’ll share coins, levels and the dashboard. Each child on their own device gets their own progress.

Purchases

What’s free and what does the Full Unlock add?

6 games are free forever: Count the Critters, Adding Fun, Shape Hunt, Letter Match, Sound Safari, Weather Watch. Plus one premium game each day as the Game of the Day.

The Full Adventure Unlock is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 22 games and K+ Challenge, and doubles the Daily Gift. There are no subscriptions and no ads, ever.

Optional extras: the Style Pack (4 exclusive buddies + 2 exclusive color themes) and the Coin Chest (500 coins).

How are purchases protected from children?

Children only ever see a friendly “Ask a grown-up” screen — never prices. Every purchase is behind the parental gate, and Apple then asks for your Face ID, Touch ID or password. With Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, purchases wait for your approval and unlock automatically once approved.

How do I restore my purchases?

Purchases normally come back on their own. If one doesn’t, open Grown-Ups (answer the question), then tap Restore Purchases — it’s also on the Premium screen. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Can my family share the unlock?

Yes. The Full Adventure Unlock and Style Pack support Family Sharing. Coin Chests are used up when bought, so they aren’t shared.

I bought the app before it became free. Do I have to pay again?

No. Everyone who bought the original paid version gets the Full Adventure Unlock automatically — no purchase and no restore needed. Open the app once while connected to the internet so it can confirm your original purchase with the App Store. If games still show as locked, tap Grown-Ups → Restore Purchases, or email me and I’ll sort it out.

I was charged but nothing unlocked.

Tap Restore Purchases in the Grown-Ups area. If the purchase was waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), it unlocks automatically once approved. Coin Chest coins are saved safely even if the app closes mid-purchase — they appear the next time you open the app. If something still looks wrong, email me.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress & privacy

Does progress sync between devices?

Progress (levels, coins, achievements and settings) is saved on the device you play on and is protected against being cleared by the system. It doesn’t sync between devices, and it’s removed if you delete the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry over to any device.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again from the App Store. This clears all progress on that device. Purchases aren’t affected — tap Restore Purchases afterwards if needed.

What data does the app collect?

None. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never connects to the internet for game content. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. Read the full privacy policy.

Accessibility

What accessibility features are included?

Read-aloud for every question and hint, with your choice of voice and reading speed. Big text (Settings → Comfort) enlarges questions and answers. Calm mode turns off confetti and reduces animations. There’s no time pressure unless you turn the timer on.

On iPad with a keyboard you can press 1–4 to choose an answer, type numbers on the number pad, and press Return to check. Answer buttons carry spoken labels for VoiceOver.
